Today, I was reading an Anthropic engineering blog about evaluating AI agents when I came across this sentence:

"When teams first start building agents, they can get surprisingly far through a combination of manual testing, dogfooding, and intuition."

Dogfooding?

I had never seen that word before, and I couldn't even guess what it had to do with software engineering.

It turns out that "dogfooding" means using your own product yourself to see how well it actually works.

So if a company builds an AI tool and its own employees use it in their daily work, that's dogfooding.

The term comes from the expression "eat your own dog food." The idea is that if you make dog food, you should be willing to eat it yourself!
