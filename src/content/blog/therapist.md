---
title: 'The Word Was "Therapist"!'
description: "When Both Languages Fail Me."
pubDate: "Sep 28 2026"
---

After work today, I met Yanzhuo in person for the first time, and we had such a great time.

At one point, we were chatting at a bar when I suddenly forgot the English word for a profession. The word just wouldn't come to me.

So I started describing it:

"Someone goes to this person and talks about their feelings and problems, and this person listens to them and helps them feel better..."

Yanzhuo immediately said, "Therapist!"

Yep. Therapist!

That was exactly the word I was looking for.

The funny part was that, I couldn't even remember how to say it in Chinese either.

I guess that's one of the struggles of being bilingual(or maybe bye-lingual?): sometimes the word you're looking for disappears in English, and somehow disappears in your native language too.

At least now I probably won't forget "therapist" again.
