title: "A Tosti at Brasserie Science"
description: "My first encounter with the Dutch tosti."
pubDate: "Sep 26 2026"
---

This Wednesday, as usual, I went to Brasserie Science in the Gorlaeus Building for lunch.

Before coming to the Netherlands, I probably would have called a place like this a "cafeteria", but Leiden University calls it a "brasserie". I guess "Brasserie Science" sounds a little nicer than "Science Cafeteria".

Anyway, I was standing in line and wasn't quite sure what to order. I saw the people in front of me ordering a "tosti", so I decided to get the same thing.

The word "tosti" was completely new to me. When I got my food, I realized that it was very similar to a grilled cheese sandwich. The outside of the bread was warm and crispy, while the cheese inside was salty and completely melted. It felt like the perfect university lunch: quick to order, quick to eat, and filling enough when you need to hurry to your next class.
