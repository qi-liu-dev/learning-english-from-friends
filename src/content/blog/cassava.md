---
title: "Cassava Is Not a Flavor"
description: "It took me way too long to realize what cassava actually is."
pubDate: "Oct 11 2026"
---

Today, I went to a friend's housewarming lunch and brought a bag of Indonesian cassava chips.

I've eaten cassava chips many times before, but somehow I had always assumed that cassava was an Indonesian word for some kind of flavor.

Apparently not.

She told me that cassava is actually a root vegetable. I was super surprised, so I googled it and found out that in Chinese, it's 木薯.

That surprised me even more because I had always thought 木薯 was called tapioca in English.

It turns out that they're not exactly the same thing.

Cassava is the plant and the root itself.

Tapioca is the starch extracted from cassava. It's also what things like tapioca pearls are made from.
