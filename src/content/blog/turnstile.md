---
title: "The Turnstile Sent Me Back Outside"
description: "A small vocabulary lesson from a buggy office turnstile."
pubDate: "Sep 29 2026"
---

After lunch, when I arrived at the office, I used my badge to enter through the turnstile as usual.

After I scanned my badge, the turnstile was supposed to rotate forward and let me in. Instead, it suddenly spun the other way and sent me right back outside.

I wanted to joke with my coworker about how the turnstile had somehow turned backwards, but then I realized I didn't actually know how to describe it in English.

Apparently, the gate is called a "turnstile".

I eventually realized that the turnstile was probably just malfunctioning. I tried another one and got in without any problem.

So today I learned:

**turnstile**: a gate with rotating arms that allows one person to pass through at a time.

And if it moves in the wrong direction, I can simply say:

**"The turnstile spun the wrong way."**

Or, in my case:

**"The turnstile sent me back outside."**
